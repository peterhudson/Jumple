    How to play

    Make words from the nine letters. Unlike Squaredle, the letters don't need to touch: jump from any letter to any other.

    SAT ORW NLE

    Tap the letters in order, or type the word, then press Enter. Words need at least 4 letters, and each letter can be used once per word. Here, T, E, N, S makes TENS.

    Earn stars

    Longer words score more. Tap the word count above the board to see your words. Rarer words count as bonus words but don't add points.

    R37

    ★ First star: a red number shows how many words still to find start with each letter.

    ★ Second star: a white number shows how many words still to find use each letter.

    A letter fades once every word that uses it is found.

    Hints

    ★ Third star: hints appear above the word list.

    Show some letters reveals the start (and, for long words, the end) of each word you haven't found yet.

    Sort words alphabetically puts the missing words in order among the ones you've found.

    💡 Reveals light up a 4, 5 or 6 letter word on the board for you to tap out. You get one per board, plus one for the third star.

    ⤨ Shuffle moves the letters around, which can help you spot new words.
